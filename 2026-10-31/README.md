## 3. 实用句子

1. **I study English for thirty minutes a day.**  
   我每天学英语三十分钟。
2. **Could you tell me how to get to the station?**  
   您能告诉我怎么去车站吗？
3. **I went to the park yesterday.**  
   我昨天去了公园。
4. **I'm going to study English next month.**  
   我下个月打算继续学英语。

## 4. 对话朗读

**A:** Hi, I'm Alex. What's your name?  
**B:** I'm Sam. Nice to meet you.  
**A:** What did you do yesterday?  
**B:** I studied English at home.  
**A:** What are you going to do next month?  
**B:** I'm going to practice speaking every day.  

先朗读两遍，再替换成自己的信息；如果方便，可以录下第二遍并回听。
