## 2. 核心词句

| 英语 | 中文 |
| --- | --- |
| turn left | 向左转 |
| next to | 在……旁边 |

## 3. 实用句子

1. **The pharmacy is next to the hotel.**  
   药店在酒店旁边。
2. **I studied English for thirty minutes yesterday.**  
   我昨天学了三十分钟英语。
3. **I walked to the station and turned left.**  
   我走到车站，然后向左转。
4. **I'm going to book a room for this weekend.**  
   我打算预订这个周末的房间。

## 4. 对话朗读

**A:** How was your trip?  
**B:** It was good. I stayed at a hotel.  
**A:** Was there a pharmacy nearby?  
**B:** Yes, it was next to the hotel.  
**A:** What are you going to do this weekend?  
**B:** I'm going to study English at home.  

先朗读两遍，再替换成自己的信息；如果方便，可以录下第二遍并回听。
