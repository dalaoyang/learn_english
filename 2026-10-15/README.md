## 第一阶段：真实旧错变式 · 5分钟

**R1.** 用括号内单词的合适形式填空：

> I learn English by ______ (read) short texts.

**R2.** 找出下面句子的**主语和主要动词**。plan意为“打算”。

> She plans to read a short text after dinner.

两题均标记把握；任选一题写一句简短依据，不清楚就写“不清楚”。先做完复习，再读下面的主任务。

## 第二阶段：阅读选择 · 20分钟

词义提示只用于理解背景：

- calendar：日历
- shelf：架子
- tidy：整洁的

### 原文

> Alex works as a programmer. He wants to pass an English exam, but he has little time after work. At first, he tried to read for an hour every evening. He often felt tired, so he stopped after a few days. Now he studies for twenty minutes after dinner. There is a small calendar on his wall, and his phone stays on a shelf. He reads a short text first. Then he reviews two old mistakes and writes one sentence about his day. On Friday, he studies with a friend. They talk about difficult questions and keep their notebooks tidy. Alex still finds some words hard. However, short lessons make it easier for him to continue studying. He thinks a small plan works better than a long plan that he cannot finish.

先读完整原文，再答题。不必逐词翻译。**每题都给答案、把握和原文依据**；依据可以抄一个短语或一句话，主旨题可以指出两处相关信息。找不到依据时写“不清楚”。
